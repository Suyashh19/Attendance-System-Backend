Delete your Attendance System account

Attendance System by Suyash Patil. You can delete your account here without the app, or in the app under Profile → Delete Account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, password, roll number, PRN and linked device ID.
  • Students: all your subject enrollments, leave requests and attendance records.
  • Faculty: all subjects you created, with their lectures, enrollments, leave requests and the attendance records of every student in them.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept afterwards, and it cannot be undone.
