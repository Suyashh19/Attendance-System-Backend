Privacy Policy

Attendance System · Effective 7 November 2026

Attendance System ("the app") is developed by Suyash Patil. It lets faculty run lectures and lets students mark their attendance with a short code shown in class. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

DataWhoWhy
Name and email addressAll usersTo create your account, sign you in and send one-time codes (OTP) for sign-up, password reset and device changes.
PasswordAll usersTo sign you in. It is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody can read it.
Roll number and PRNStudentsTo identify you to your faculty on attendance lists and reports.
Device ID (Android ID)All usersTo link your account to your phone. For students, this stops attendance being marked for someone else from another phone.
Attendance recordsStudentsPresent/absent/invalid status, the code you entered, when you submitted it and, if your attendance was marked invalid, the reason, for each lecture.
Activity during the attendance windowStudentsOnly while the attendance window is open (10 to 15 seconds), the app checks whether a phone call (including a call in an app such as WhatsApp) is active on your phone and whether you leave the app. If either happens, your attendance for that lecture is marked invalid with that reason, which you and your faculty can see. The app never sees who you called, your call history or call content, or which other apps you used, and nothing is checked outside the attendance window.
Leave requestsStudentsThe subjects, dates and reason you give when you apply for leave, and your faculty's answer and note. Your faculty see them so they can approve or decline, and classes missed on approved leave are marked as excused.
Subjects, enrollments and lecturesFaculty and studentsTo run the attendance features of the app.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs or phone numbers, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Preferences you choose in the app, such as the semester months, are kept on your phone only and are not sent to our servers. The exception is the attendance time a faculty member picks (10 to 15 seconds), which is sent with each lecture they start so every student gets the same window.

How your data is used and shared

  • Your data is used only to provide the app's attendance features.
  • Faculty can see the name, roll number, PRN and attendance of students enrolled in their subjects, and the leave requests those students send for their subjects. They can export attendance as reports.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or other third parties.
  • We use these service providers to run the app, who process data only on our behalf: Render (server hosting), Neon (database hosting) and Resend (sending OTP emails).
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

Security

All data travels between the app and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, and your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. One-time codes expire within minutes. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account
  • On the web: delete your account here

Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account and its data are removed from our database. If you are faculty, your subjects and their lectures, enrollments, leave requests and attendance records are deleted too. If you are a student, your leave requests are deleted along with your attendance records.

Children

The app is meant for college students and faculty. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
