Privacy Policy

Attendance System · Effective 17 October 2026

Attendance System ("the app") is developed by Suyash Patil. It lets faculty run lectures and lets students mark their attendance with a short code shown in class. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

DataWhoWhy
Name and email addressAll usersTo create your account, sign you in and send one-time codes (OTP) for sign-up, password reset and device changes.
PasswordAll usersTo sign you in. It is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody can read it.
Roll number and PRNStudentsTo identify you to your faculty on attendance lists and reports.
Device ID (Android ID)StudentsTo link your account to one phone, so attendance cannot be marked for someone else from another phone.
Attendance recordsStudentsPresent/absent status, the code you entered and when you submitted it, for each lecture.
Subjects, enrollments and lecturesFaculty and studentsTo run the attendance features of the app.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How your data is used and shared

  • Your data is used only to provide the app's attendance features.
  • Faculty can see the name, roll number, PRN and attendance of students enrolled in their subjects, and can export these as attendance reports.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or other third parties.
  • We use these service providers to run the app, who process data only on our behalf: Render (server hosting), Neon (database hosting) and Resend (sending OTP emails).
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

Security

All data travels between the app and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, and your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. One-time codes expire within minutes. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account
  • On the web: delete your account here

Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account and its data are removed from our database. If you are faculty, your subjects and their lectures, enrollments and attendance records are deleted too.

Children

The app is meant for college students and faculty. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
